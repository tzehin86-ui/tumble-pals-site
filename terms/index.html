Stated plainly, because this is the only item that charges you again by itself:

Length 1 month (Google Play base plan P1M; Apple auto-renewable, monthly)
Price US$4.99 per month (or the equivalent local price shown in the store)
What it includes Premium pass track, no interstitial ads, daily rewarded-video gem bonus 30 to 60 (other gem sources unchanged), 1.5× daily login coins, one free run boost each day

The 30-day Challenge Pass is not a subscription. It is a one-off purchase that simply expires and never charges you again.

4.4 Quiet Mode is not "remove ads"

Quiet Mode (US$3.99, one-time) turns off the full-screen interstitial ads you did not ask for. It deliberately keeps every rewarded video and everything they give you — ad revive, the daily gem video, the piggy-bank boost. We say this here and in the store description because an earlier product that removed all ads was discontinued for exactly this reason: it quietly took away the free gem taps, so the people who paid ended up with less than the people who did not.

4.7 Parental controls

A parental gate (an arithmetic question) is shown before any real-money purchase. This is a speed bump, not a security control. To block purchases outright, use your device's own parental controls — Apple "Ask to Buy" / Screen Time, or Google Play's purchase authentication.

Gems bought with real money are gated too. The Game tracks how much of your gem balance came from a purchase, spends free gems first, and shows the parental gate for any single spend that would use more than 250 purchased gems. A grown-up can switch on "gem spending needs a grown-up" to gate every gem spend.

If the age you gave the Game is under 13 — or you never answered — the store hides everything above US$9.99 and hides the auto-renewing subscription entirely. Every content path remains buyable below that cap.

6. Ads

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. There are two kinds and both are described in full in the privacy policy, section 1a: rewarded videos, which are always optional, and interstitials, which are not. Interstitials are capped at 3 per session and 6 per day; rewarded videos that top up coins are capped at 12 per day, and there is a second, absolute ceiling of 20 rewarded videos per day across every placement — revive, the daily gem video and the piggy-bank boost included. Those caps are enforced in the app and survive closing the Game.

Rewarded videos are capped per play session as well: the coin top-up prompt stops after 4 in one sitting, and 8 in one sitting is an absolute ceiling across every placement. A daily cap alone does not stop one long sitting from being dense with ads. Session counts reset when you close and reopen the Game; the daily ceilings above do not.

Which ads you see depends on the age you tell the Game once, at first launch (privacy policy, section 2b). If you do not answer, the stricter child-directed settings apply.
